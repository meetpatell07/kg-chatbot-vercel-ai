# TechCorp Cloud Services - Frequently Asked Questions

## Product Overview

### What is TechCorp Cloud Services?
TechCorp Cloud Services is a comprehensive cloud computing platform that provides scalable infrastructure, storage solutions, and managed services for businesses of all sizes. Our platform offers reliable, secure, and cost-effective cloud solutions to help organizations modernize their IT infrastructure.

### What services does TechCorp Cloud offer?
We offer three main service categories:
1. **Compute Services**: Virtual machines, containers, and serverless computing options
2. **Storage Solutions**: Object storage, block storage, and file storage with automatic backups
3. **Managed Services**: Database hosting, monitoring, security, and 24/7 technical support

### Who is TechCorp Cloud Services designed for?
Our platform is designed for businesses ranging from startups to enterprise-level organizations. Whether you're a small business looking to migrate to the cloud or a large enterprise needing scalable infrastructure, TechCorp Cloud Services can accommodate your needs.

## Pricing and Billing

### How does pricing work?
TechCorp Cloud Services uses a pay-as-you-go pricing model. You only pay for the resources you use, with no upfront costs or long-term commitments required. Pricing varies based on:
- Compute resources (CPU, RAM, and storage)
- Data transfer and bandwidth usage
- Additional services like managed databases or premium support

### Are there any free tiers available?
Yes! We offer a free tier that includes:
- 750 hours of compute time per month
- 5GB of object storage
- 1GB of database storage
- Basic monitoring and support

### How can I estimate my monthly costs?
You can use our cost calculator on the website, or contact our sales team for a personalized quote. We also provide detailed billing dashboards that show real-time usage and projected costs.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Enterprise customers can also set up invoicing with net-30 payment terms.

## Security and Compliance

### How secure is TechCorp Cloud Services?
Security is our top priority. We implement multiple layers of security including:
- End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Compliance with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR standards
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all accounts
- DDoS protection and automated threat detection

### Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored in our geographically distributed data centers located in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific regions. You can choose your preferred data region during setup, and we ensure data residency compliance for regulated industries.

### Do you offer compliance certifications?
Yes, TechCorp Cloud Services is compliant with:
- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO 27001
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- HIPAA (for healthcare customers with appropriate agreements)
- PCI DSS Level 1 (for payment processing)

## Migration and Setup

### How do I get started?
Getting started is simple:
1. Create a free account on our website
2. Verify your email address
3. Add a payment method (required for paid services)
4. Launch your first instance using our web console or API
5. Follow our getting started guide for step-by-step instructions

### Can I migrate my existing infrastructure?
Absolutely! We offer free migration assistance for new customers. Our team can help you:
- Assess your current infrastructure
- Plan the migration strategy
- Execute the migration with minimal downtime
- Provide post-migration support and optimization

### How long does setup take?
For basic services, you can be up and running in minutes. For complex enterprise setups with custom configurations, our team typically completes setup within 2-5 business days.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### What is your uptime guarantee?
We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all services, with 99.99% uptime available for Enterprise customers. If we fail to meet these guarantees, we provide service credits as outlined in our SLA.

### What happens if there's downtime?
In the event of service disruption, we:
1. Immediately notify all affected customers via email and status page
2. Provide regular updates every 30 minutes until resolution
3. Post a detailed incident report within 24 hours
4. Issue service credits according to our SLA terms

## Account Management

### Can I cancel my account at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your account at any time with no cancellation fees. Your services will remain active until the end of your current billing period, and you'll retain access to your data during this time.

### How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your account dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated accordingly.

### Can I have multiple projects or teams?
Yes! Our platform supports multiple projects and team collaboration. You can:
- Create separate projects for different applications
- Invite team members with role-based access control
- Set up billing groups for different departments
- Use organization accounts for enterprise customers
